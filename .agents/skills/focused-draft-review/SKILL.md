# Focused Draft Review

Improve what the draft needs to accomplish, not its conformity to a house style. Keep private diagnosis separate from the text readers will see.

## Bind version and purpose

Read the complete relevant draft and current request. Establish the reader, medium, central point or experience, and approved material from available context. Ask only about a gap that changes the edit. Resolve conflicting versions before replacing text.

Apply confirmed changes, not rejected options, hypothetical suggestions, or a later aside that silently replaces the goal. A review-only request calls for findings, not an unsolicited rewrite.

## Find the consequential failure

Locate the earliest failure with a concrete passage and reader consequence: a missing reasoning bridge, unsupported claim, case stripped of causal steps, repeated conclusion, viewpoint drift, or medium mismatch. Use only lenses the draft needs.

Distinguish a faulty premise or evidence gap from local wording. Do not manufacture criticism to fill a checklist, use an AI-detector score as a verdict, or treat a punctuation habit as proof of poor authorship. If the draft works, say so.

For fiction, evaluate events through its established world, character knowledge, motives, and circumstances. Do not insert modern procedures, moral lessons, punishment, or orderly behavior merely to make the world reassuring. Never import another project's characters or facts.

## Repair at the right scale

When editing is requested, preserve the sound argument, facts, voice, and emotional force. Fix the failure and its actual dependencies. Removing repetition must not remove the only explanation that makes the conclusion follow. Express necessary uncertainty naturally, without generic disclaimers or a ritual opposing view.

A private reply may need one continuous speaking action; a long argument may need several steps. Neither is the universal template. Do not mistake shortness for clarity or length for depth.

Rebuild the whole draft only when its premise or structure cannot support the goal, or the user requests it. Briefly explain that structural issue instead of polishing around it. An alternative direction remains a proposal until adopted.

## Check the actual revision

Compare original and revision for claims, certainty, chronology, important examples, approved wording, and unresolved questions. Restore accidental losses. Verify material facts with available sources or identify the precise unresolved claim; never invent citations. Keep this check backstage unless it changes the user's decision.

For ready-to-send requests, return clean text alone. When review and revision are both requested, distinguish the deliverable from a brief explanation. Keep workflow labels, scores, and editing instructions out of the finished piece. Do not publish, change canon, or overwrite files without authorization.
